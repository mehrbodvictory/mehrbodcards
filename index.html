0/12 UNITS

Any rarity can go in your deck, but only Blue can ever be placed directly onto the board. A higher-tier pick becomes a "blueprint" — merging two Blues (or fusing further) requires a matching blueprint still sitting unused in your deck, and consumes it to flavor the result. No blueprint for a tier in your deck means you can never merge into that tier, so pick some Green/Red/Orange cards if you want to fuse up. Everything you pick is visible and available from round 1 - there's no draw step.
